## Background

The fresh produce market in the United States was characterized by a growing demand for convenient and healthy food options in the early 2010s. Consumers were increasingly seeking out products that were free from preservatives and additives, and that offered a high level of nutritional value. The rise of meal kit delivery services, such as Blue Apron and HelloFresh, had also created a new market for pre-portioned ingredients and recipes.

However, these services often required consumers to handle and store perishable ingredients, which could be time-consuming and inconvenient. Additionally, the quality of the ingredients could vary depending on the supplier and the handling process. This created an opportunity for a company that could provide a convenient and healthy food option that was also easy to use and required minimal handling.

### Key Trends and Conditions

* Growing demand for healthy and convenient food options
* Rise of meal kit delivery services
* Increasing consumer awareness of food quality and safety
* Growing demand for pre-portioned ingredients and recipes

## Founding Story

Juicero was founded in 2014 by Doug Evans and Ryan Sweeney. Evans, a serial entrepreneur and food enthusiast, had previously founded a company called Evolution Fresh, which was later acquired by Starbucks. Sweeney, a former executive at Apple, had a background in product development and marketing.

The two founders met while working on a project to develop a new type of juicer that could extract juice from whole fruits and vegetables. They saw an opportunity to create a product that could provide a convenient and healthy food option that was also easy to use and required minimal handling.

### Founding Team

* Doug Evans: Founder and CEO
* Ryan Sweeney: Co-Founder and Chief Product Officer
* Other key team members: Adam Anders, Chief Marketing Officer; Jeff Foster, Chief Financial Officer

## Product Development

Juicero's initial product was a high-end juicer that used a proprietary technology to extract juice from whole fruits and vegetables. The juicer was designed to be compact and easy to use, with a touchscreen interface and a built-in scale.

The company's initial product was launched in 2016, and it quickly gained attention for its sleek design and innovative technology. However, the product was also expensive, with a price tag of $399.

### Key Features and Technology

* Compact and easy-to-use design
* Touchscreen interface and built-in scale
* High-end materials and finishes

Juicero's legacy is one of a failed experiment in the food and beverage industry. The company's innovative technology and sleek design were not enough to overcome its high customer acquisition costs and slow revenue growth.
