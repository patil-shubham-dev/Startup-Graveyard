## The Record — Why this file exists

Better Home & Finance Holding Company (NASDAQ: BETR) is still operating. This
archive documents failures; Better is not one — so this case file is rejected
and unpublished. It is retained for two reasons:

1. The inbound pipeline lists Better.com as a candidate company; this row and
   file act as a permanent guard against generation of a fictional "shutdown"
   case.
2. The company's near-death episode (2021-2022) is one of the best-documented
   blitzscaling disasters in startup history. It is worth keeping on record —
   with sources — for the day a real verdict is possible.

## The Episode (facts, verified 2026-08-08)

- **2016** — Founded by Vishal Garg (solo founder) in New York. Thesis: strip
  cost and friction out of US mortgage origination with an online-only
  brokerage that is "truly better".
- **2017-2020** — Grows through the refinancing boom. Raises ~$905M across 9
  rounds (~$30M Series A 2016; ~$15M Series B 2017; ~$160M Series C led by
  Activant Capital, Jan 2019; ~$200M Series D led by L Catterton at ~$4B
  valuation, Nov 2020).
- **April 2021** — SoftBank (SB Management) buys $500M of existing shares
  (secondary) at a $6B valuation. Peak private valuation: $7.7B; peak
  headcount about 10,000.
- **May 2021** — SPAC announced: merger with Aurora Acquisition Corp.
  (Novator Capital) at a claimed $7.7B post-equity value with a ~$1.5B PIPE
  led by a SoftBank subsidiary (CNBC, HousingWire, TechCrunch, 2021-05-11).
  The deal takes over two years to close.
- **December 1, 2021** — About 900 employees (9%) laid off on a Zoom call;
  a leaked recording shows Garg's combative language. Becomes a defining
  tech-layoffs moment; Garg later apologizes, and complaints about the
  severance terms draw further criticism.
- **2022** — Interest-rate shock ends the refi boom; further rounds of cuts
  follow. Quarterly losses run into nine digits. The SPAC keeps slipping.
  The 2021-22 sequence is why outsiders (including this archive's original
  draft) believed Better had died.
- **July 2023** — The Aurora SPAC finally closes; BETR begins trading.
- **2024-2025** — Rationalization: headcount down to roughly 1,500-2,000;
  listed equity trades at a fraction of the 2021 marks. The company hinges
  its turnaround on its proprietary "Tinman" AI origination engine and
  launches better.com on a direct-to-consumer basis (among the first US
  lenders in years to do so).
- **2026** — Still public, still growing: Q2 2026 funded volume $1.67B (+38%
  YoY), revenue $54.7M (+28% YoY), net loss narrowed to $30.6M, adjusted
  EBITDA -$14.0M. Cumulative funded volume tops $110B. In July 2026 Garg
  steps down as CEO; the Board appoints Daniel Lewis interim CEO. The stock
  has slid about 45% in three months and press coverage cites a securities
  investigation (Simply Wall St, Aug 2026). The company is streamlining:
  selling its UK bank (Birmingham) as discontinued operations, cutting
  ~$25M annualized costs, and expanding its warehouse facility from $575M to
  $850M.

## Why it is not published

- A shutdown_year for Better would be fabricated — the company is not shut
  down.
- This archive's bar is "evidence over opinion" and documented failures. A
  still-operating public company that grew revenue 28% YoY in its latest
  quarter does not belong in a register of the dead.
- If it eventually dies, this record plus the 2021-2026 timeline is the
  scaffold for a proper case file.
